Hay Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts hay with division and rounding-up: the pounds a herd eats a day, the days a stack of bales lasts, the room the bales take, and the cost a ton. The head count, the pounds a day, the bale weight and size and the prices are yours to weigh and measure off your own scale and seller ticket. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your animals and does not know their needs, their health, the hay's quality or your vet's advice, and it gives no verdict that an amount of hay is enough or right for any animal. Every number here is your own entry.

The feed truth

Feed: the head times the pounds a day, over the bale weight.

The days truth

Days: the pounds in the bales divided by the pounds a day, rounded down.

The room truth

Room: the bale size times the count, and the floor the stacks cover.

The cost truth

Cost: the bales at their price plus delivery, and what that is a ton.

Hay Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
